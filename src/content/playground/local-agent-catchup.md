The first playground experiment is a daily catchup generated from a local agentic workflow. The goal is not to produce more AI news, but to make a smaller, more useful signal: what changed, why it matters, and what deserves a deeper look.

The public feed will be curated before publishing. It will never expose private prompts, local files, credentials, or confidential work discussions.
