why: Architecture decisions make more sense when they solve a real problem. 
without: We may build an impressive demo that nobody needs, or optimize the wrong technical problem. Our public chatbot should answer product questions from approved company information, explain the available solutions, and help create a support request when a person needs more help.
role: We define the visitors, the customer job, the expected answer, and the boundary between public guidance and account-specific action before choosing components.
tradeoff: A clear scope limits the first version, but it gives the team a useful target and a way to measure progress.
question: What should the chatbot help a customer accomplish, and what must remain a controlled support action?
