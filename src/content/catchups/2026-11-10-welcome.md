I am experimenting with a local group of agents that helps me follow developments in AI. The interesting question is not whether an agent can summarize a link. It is whether a system can help me decide what is worth reading, show where its confidence comes from, and remember the questions that remain open.

This catchup is the first public note from that experiment. The format will evolve as I learn what is genuinely useful.
