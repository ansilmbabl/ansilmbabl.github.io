## The problem

Many HR support questions were repetitive but depended on finding the right information and applying the correct workflow for the person asking.

## My contribution

I developed and deployed an internal assistant using LLMs, async Python, FastAPI, Pydantic, structured outputs, document retrieval, role-based access control, retries, fallbacks, and workflow automation.

The system was designed to provide useful answers while respecting access boundaries and handling failures explicitly instead of presenting uncertain output as fact.

## Outcome

The assistant reduced repetitive HR support queries by approximately 40%. Implementation details are intentionally kept high level because this was internal work.
