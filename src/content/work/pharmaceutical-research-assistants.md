## The problem

Pharmaceutical research workflows required teams to search across information sources, interpret changing requirements, and repeatedly prepare analysis for recruitment, demand, and inventory decisions.

## My contribution

I built and integrated Generative AI assistants using LLMs, vector databases, retrieval-augmented generation, and agentic workflows. The assistants connected research context with focused workflow steps rather than returning unstructured chat responses.

## Outcome

The systems reduced human effort and analysis time by approximately 50%, giving research teams a faster way to work through recurring information-heavy tasks.
