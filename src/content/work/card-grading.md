## The problem

Trading-card grading was slow and required physical submission. The aim was to make an initial assessment possible from a mobile image.

## My contribution

I combined segmentation, object detection, OCR, and LLM-based analysis into a grading and price-estimation workflow. Custom grading models were made 80% smaller and more accurate than the traditional models used as a baseline.

## Outcome

Assessment time fell from approximately five minutes to seconds, enabling instant grading through mobile image capture.
